# Casehorizon — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Lab facilitator / academy lead

- As a facilitator, I want to instantiate an Ottawa-style agenda with domain case packs preloaded, so that I am not rebuilding canvases from scratch each city.
- As an academy lead, I want cross-domain synthesis after breakouts, so that participants learn from housing and immigration in one cohort.
- As a facilitator, I want the lab to refuse close-out until top recommendations are ranked and owned, so that sticky notes do not die on the wall.

### Emerging policy leader participant

- As a participant, I want prompts for who is harmed and helped, so that efficiency claims cannot silence distributional effects.
- As a participant, I want immigration and hiring cases to force procedural-fairness questions, so that backlog reduction is not the only frame.
- As a participant, I want to see how my recommendation maps to the TBS Directive language, so that I leave with an actionable federal hook.

### Departmental responsible-AI / AIA lead

- As an AIA lead, I want lab recommendations that propose automated decisions to open a phase-gate record, so that foresight connects to assurance.
- As a department lead, I want orphan recommendations aged in a report, so that lab theatre is visible to my ADM.
- As an assurance advisor, I want to reject a recommendation adoption that claims AIA completion without evidence, so that gates stay meaningful.

### Platform administrator

- As an administrator, I want case packs free of live personal data by design, so that a lab cannot become a privacy incident.
- As an administrator, I want published outputs marked as participant exercise unless formally adopted, so that CIFAR/BII+E-style disclaimers are enforced.
